Celestia’s v4 mainnet upgrade, Lotus, is on the horizon. Lotus integrates Hyperlane as a Cosmos SDK module. Hyperlane enables direct TIA interop for new Celestia rollups and non-Celestia chains with Hyperlane deployments, such as Ethereum Mainnet, Base, Arbitrum One, and more.
